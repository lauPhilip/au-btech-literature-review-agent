# SCImago Journal Rank data

The CSV files in this folder (`scimagojr 2020.csv` to `scimagojr 2025.csv`) are the yearly journal rankings
published by SCImago at <https://www.scimagojr.com/journalrank.php>. TraceableAI uses them only to look up the
quartile (Q1 to Q4) of an included paper's journal by exact title and year.

**These files are not covered by the Apache License of this repository.** SCImago's terms say that the
information on the SCImago Journal & Country Rank website "can be used for non-commercial purposes as long as
it is cited". They are included for non-commercial research use under those terms. If you use TraceableAI
commercially, remove these files (the quartile then shows as N/A) or obtain permission from SCImago.

Cite the data as:

> SCImago. (n.d.). *SJR — SCImago Journal & Country Rank* [Portal]. Retrieved from http://www.scimagojr.com

The generated review names SJR as the source of the quartiles in its availability statement.
