# Components/Paper

The parts of a review's paper page that every kind of review shares (module design M9). `CitedProse` shows a text with every [n] as a button; clicking it opens what the citation check found for that sentence and source (its verdict, the quote and where it was found), and the page adds its own extras to the open panel, such as reviewer notes and a re-check. `CitationAttentionBar` is the bar at the top of the paper with the citations that need the reader's attention and buttons to step through them. `CitationView` holds the verdicts' labels and colours, the ids of the citation numbers and the reading order. The page owns which citation is open and moves the focus; these components only show it. The systematic review's Review output page (`Pages/SpecMatrix.razor`) uses them, and the multivocal review's paper page will.
