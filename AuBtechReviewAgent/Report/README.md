# Report

Everything the run hands over. `PrismaReviewEngine.Report.cs` fetches the full texts, extracts the studies and numbers the references, and `PrismaReviewEngine.ReportDraft.cs` writes the PRISMA 2020 report in named steps: the outline and first draft there, the synthesis and discussion in `PrismaReviewEngine.CitedSections.cs`, the front matter, artifact and closing sections in `PrismaReviewEngine.ReportFrontMatter.cs` (the closing sections beside the artifact, then the front matter, which gets their answers and conclusion), and the citation check, coverage (counted over the results and the discussion only) and report files in `PrismaReviewEngine.ReportCitations.cs`. `MethodsSectionWriter.cs` (PRISMA items 5 to 8) with `MethodsSectionWriter.Studies.cs` (the items on the included studies and the MMAT notes), `PrismaFlowDiagram.cs` and `AiUseStatement.cs` write the methods, the flow diagram and the statement on AI use in code, from what the run actually did, never by the model. `ApaCitationBuilder.cs` (the author part in `ApaCitationBuilder.Authors.cs`) and `JournalRankingMatcher.cs` build the references and venue ranks from the database metadata, with the volume, issue, pages, publisher and arXiv number held in `ReferenceDetails.cs`, and `BibliographyExporter.cs` writes BibTeX and RIS with the same details. `PrismaReviewEngine.References.cs` fills the gaps in the included studies' references before they are numbered: `ReferenceEnricher.cs` asks Crossref, OpenAlex or arXiv by DOI or arXiv number and saves each answer, `ReferenceAnswers.cs` reads them, and `ReferenceMerge.cs` fills the record and writes the reference again, with every filled field named in `reference-enrichment.json`. `CitationMarkers.cs` sets the in-text citations of main.tex sorted and ranged ("[1–4]"); the run's files keep one number per study. `RunArchive.cs` builds the downloadable archive for every kind of review: the files every run has, the source texts and answers, and the module's own files; `RunManifest.cs` fingerprints every file in it. `ReviewPaper.cs` is the paper of every kind of review in one shape (title, abstract, introduction, method, data, results by theme, discussion, declarations, references, and an optional appendix after them), and `PaperLatex` writes it as `main.tex`; a module fills the shape and puts its own parts (method sections, figures, tables, evidence map) into its LaTeX slots, drawn in code. `PrismaReviewEngine.Archive.cs` fills it for a systematic review and adds the bibliographies; its figures are drawn in `PrismaReviewEngine.ArchiveFigures.cs` and its evidence map, artifact and appendix tables in `PrismaReviewEngine.ArchiveTables.cs`. `ReportEdits.cs` puts the reviewer's checked sentence edits into a copy of the report, in code, for main.tex, the page and the API, and leaves a run without edits exactly as it was. `RunArchiveCache.cs` keeps a finished run's archive in its folder, so a repeat download does not build it again.
