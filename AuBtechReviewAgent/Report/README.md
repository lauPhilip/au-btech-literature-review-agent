# Report

Everything the run hands over. `PrismaReviewEngine.Report.cs` fetches the full texts, extracts the studies and numbers the references, and `PrismaReviewEngine.ReportDraft.cs` writes the PRISMA 2020 report in named steps: the outline and first draft there, the synthesis and discussion in `PrismaReviewEngine.CitedSections.cs`, the front matter, artifact and closing sections in `PrismaReviewEngine.ReportFrontMatter.cs`, and the citation check, coverage and report files in `PrismaReviewEngine.ReportCitations.cs`. `MethodsSectionWriter.cs` (PRISMA items 5 to 8) with `MethodsSectionWriter.Studies.cs` (the items on the included studies and the MMAT notes), `PrismaFlowDiagram.cs` and `AiUseStatement.cs` write the methods, the flow diagram and the statement on AI use in code, from what the run actually did, never by the model. `ApaCitationBuilder.cs` and `JournalRankingMatcher.cs` build the references and venue ranks from the database metadata, and `BibliographyExporter.cs` writes BibTeX and RIS. `RunArchive.cs` builds the downloadable archive for every kind of review: the files every run has, the source texts and answers, and the module's own files; `RunManifest.cs` fingerprints every file in it. `ReviewPaper.cs` is the paper of every kind of review in one shape (title, abstract, introduction, method, data, results by theme, discussion, declarations, references, and an optional appendix after them), and `PaperLatex` writes it as `main.tex`; a module fills the shape and puts its own parts (method sections, figures, tables, evidence map) into its LaTeX slots, drawn in code. `PrismaReviewEngine.Archive.cs` fills it for a systematic review and adds the bibliographies; its figures are drawn in `PrismaReviewEngine.ArchiveFigures.cs` and its evidence map, artifact and appendix tables in `PrismaReviewEngine.ArchiveTables.cs`. `ReportEdits.cs` puts the reviewer's checked sentence edits into a copy of the report, in code, for main.tex, the page and the API, and leaves a run without edits exactly as it was. `RunArchiveCache.cs` keeps a finished run's archive in its folder, so a repeat download does not build it again.
